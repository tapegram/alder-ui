al-popover

A floating panel anchored to its invoker. placement picks the side, offset the gap, anchor another anchor by id; the browser flips it when there is no room.

Without JavaScript

popovertarget and the popover attribute are the platform's: no script runs. (Only the placement needs a browser with CSS anchor positioning.)

Opened by popovertarget. Escape or a click outside closes it.
